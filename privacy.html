Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 2026

Overview

This website provides free financial calculators and a mapping tool to help you think through housing and investment decisions. We are committed to being straightforward about data: this site does not create user accounts, does not require you to submit personal information, and does not run a server that stores your calculator inputs. All values you enter into a calculator are saved only in your own browser's local storage and never leave your device. The one exception is the Commute Map tool, which by necessity sends the addresses and search area you enter to outside mapping services — see "Commute Map & Mapping Data" below for exactly what that involves.

That said, like most websites, we use a standard third-party analytics service that collects certain information automatically. This policy explains what that means for you.

Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit:

  • Your name, email address, or any other contact information
  • Your calculator inputs (home price, income, rent, savings, etc.)
  • Financial account information of any kind
  • Precise location data

The one exception: the Commute Map tool sends the addresses you enter to Mapbox, and the resulting search area to the U.S. Census Bureau, in order to calculate travel times and matching ZIP codes. That data goes directly from your browser to those services — we don't receive, log, or store any of it ourselves. See "Commute Map & Mapping Data" below.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit this site, third-party services we use may automatically collect certain technical information, including:

  • Your IP address (used in anonymized or aggregated form)
  • Browser type and version
  • Device type and operating system
  • Pages visited and time spent on each
  • The URL of the page that referred you to this site
  • General geographic region (country or state level)

This information is collected through cookies and similar tracking technologies and is used to understand how visitors use the site.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our tools — for example, which calculators are used most and how long visitors engage with them. Google Analytics collects data in anonymized, aggregated form. We do not use this data to identify individual visitors.

Google Analytics may use cookies and may transmit data to Google servers in the United States. Google's use of this data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

You can opt out of Google Analytics tracking across all websites by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Commute Map & Mapping Data

The Commute Map tool works differently from the calculators on this site. Finding coordinates for an address, calculating a drive/walk-time area, and matching that area to ZIP codes all require live mapping data that can't be computed in your browser alone, so that page sends data directly to two outside services:

  • Mapbox — receives the addresses you type or select, for geocoding, address suggestions, and calculating drive/walk-time areas. See the Mapbox Privacy Policy.
  • U.S. Census Bureau (TIGERweb) — receives the shape of your computed search area, used only to find which ZIP codes intersect it.

These requests go directly from your browser to Mapbox and the Census Bureau — we do not see, log, or store this data ourselves, and neither service is used anywhere else on this site.

Affiliate Links

This site may contain links to third-party services — such as mortgage lenders, real estate platforms, or financial tools. If you click one of these links and make a purchase or submit an inquiry, we may receive a commission at no additional cost to you.

These third parties operate their own websites and have their own privacy policies and data practices, which we do not control. We only link to services we believe are relevant and useful in the context of the calculators on this site.

Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored in your browser. This site does not set first-party cookies for tracking purposes. Google Analytics sets its own cookies as described in the section above.

Most browsers allow you to refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Disabling cookies may affect third-party features such as analytics, but will not affect the calculators on this site, which rely on your browser's local storage rather than cookies.

Children's Privacy

This site is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, please reach out and we will take prompt steps to remove it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or applicable law. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this page periodically. Your continued use of the site after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
